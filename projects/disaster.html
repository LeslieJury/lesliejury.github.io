IHACK 2024 / HARDWARE / FIRMWARE

Freak Disasters

A gamified disaster-training program created for IHack 2024. My team built a more hands-on way to practice responding to disasters such as tornadoes and hurricanes, while I focused on the hardware and firmware side of the project.

Project demonstration

Building the prototype

We combined a software experience with physical controls so the training felt more interactive. I designed the KiCad PCB and worked on the electronics that connected the physical components to the program.
