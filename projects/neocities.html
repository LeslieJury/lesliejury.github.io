SMALL PROJECT / HTML / CSS

Neocities Website

A small website experiment built to explore handmade web design, playful layouts, and the creative freedom of building a site from scratch.

Why I made it

This project gave me a space to experiment with visual ideas outside of a traditional portfolio. I used it to practice structuring pages, styling interfaces, and making a website feel personal through color, typography, and small details.

What I practiced

Front-end foundations

Building pages with semantic HTML and reusable CSS patterns.

Creative direction

Trying different layouts, colors, and visual treatments to shape a distinct identity.

Independent iteration

Learning by making quick changes, checking the result, and refining the experience.
