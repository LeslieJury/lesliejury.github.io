Umamusume Productivity Helper

A themed productivity web app built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I wanted to turn a simple task workflow into something more motivating and playful while practicing dynamic page behavior.

What I built

The app combines a game-inspired visual system with practical productivity features. It gave me practice structuring a small web app, responding to user input, and keeping the interface visually consistent across different states.
