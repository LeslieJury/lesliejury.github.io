Cyberdeck

Here's a video demonstration of my project

In this project, I made a Cyberdeck using a Raspberry Pi running a Raspberry Pi Debian fork called Armbian. I also designed the layout of the setup and I'm prototyping right now with different boxes and peripherials. My original case was a shoe box but I upgraded to a small tin lunchbox which is a lot better!
