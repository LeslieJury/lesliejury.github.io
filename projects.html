✦ PROJECTS

CYBERDECK (RASPBERRY PI)

I made a cyberdeck using a Raspberry Pi, Linux (Armbian OS), and Peripherals. I'm still in the prototyping stage, I'm planning on improving the design and structure.

LIGHTSTICK (STM32 NUCLEO)

I utilized C and the STM32 Nucleo Microcontroller to make an interactive lightstick that flashes to the BPM of your favorite songs.

HEXCODE GAME (ARDUINO & PI)

For I-Hack 2023 We made a hexadecimal game where one adjusts the Red, Green, and Blue values using potentiometers. I was in charge of the hardware aspects of this project.

KARAOKE MACHINE (AUDIO AMPLIFIER)

In this project, I designed and fabricated a printed circuit board (PCB) for an audio amplifier. I made factory ready gerber files and sent them to a manufacturer. The most interesting step to me was the routing.
